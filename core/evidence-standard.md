# Evidence and Uncertainty Standard

Every material conclusion must remain traceable to evidence.

## Required labels

- **CONFIRMED** — directly supported by an employer, recruiter or other authoritative source.
- **PROBABLE** — strong multi-source convergence, but not directly confirmed.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — plausible but insufficiently established.
- **UNKNOWN** — insufficient evidence.

Never convert a hypothesis into a confirmed fact merely to complete a record.

## Provenance

For material claims preserve where available:

- source name;
- source URL;
- source type;
- publication/posting date;
- retrieval/discovery date;
- evidence excerpt or identifying clue;
- claim supported.

## Independence

Copies of the same original advert are not independent corroboration.

Prefer independent evidence such as:

- recruiter advert;
- employer ATS/careers page;
- employer or hiring-manager post;
- corporate information;
- leadership evidence.

## Contradictions

Contradictory evidence must be preserved, not omitted.

When a leading employer hypothesis exists, actively ask what evidence would show it is wrong.
