# Disqualifiers

## Purpose

This file answers:

> **What should the general-market commercial pipeline exclude or stop spending time on?**

Detailed classification remains controlled by the authoritative role taxonomy.

## Hard general-market exclusions

- ERP-specialist work, including SAP, Oracle ERP, Microsoft Dynamics, NetSuite and ERP implementation/support/functional consulting/development/architecture/programme work;
- Cybersecurity and security-specialist work, including SOC, IAM, PAM, AppSec, Cloud Security, Cyber Risk and security GRC;
- traditional Sales and Business Development;
- frontline Customer / Client Service and Customer Success;
- Financial Advice / Financial Planning;
- Administration / Office Support / clerical and executive-support roles;
- roles at or below the normal R420,000 p.a. threshold, unless an explicit exception applies.

## Important inclusions that must not be accidentally excluded

- Commercial Management;
- Commercial Finance;
- Pricing / Revenue Management;
- Credit Risk;
- Enterprise / Operational / Financial / Regulatory Risk;
- IT Audit when fundamentally audit/assurance;
- Cloud Engineering and Cloud Architecture;
- CRM Technology / Salesforce;
- Data Governance;
- specialist and senior individual-contributor roles;
- relevant consulting / advisory roles that are not themselves in excluded ERP or Cybersecurity branches.

## Agreed-client exception

Do not suppress a meaningful vacancy at an agreed client merely because the general-market pipeline would normally exclude it.

Route it for agreed-client review.

## Discovery vs exclusion

The LinkedIn channel remains role-agnostic during discovery.

A disqualifier controls downstream qualification and research effort. It must not be used to pretend a discovered signal never existed.

## Stop rule

Once a general-market role is clearly excluded, stop expensive employer-attribution, stakeholder and contact research unless:

- the employer is an agreed client / agreed group entity; or
- another explicit commercial exception applies.
