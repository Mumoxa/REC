# Ideal Client / Qualifying Job Profile

## Purpose

This file answers one question:

> **What does a commercially valuable Talent Tree opportunity look like?**

It is the canonical human-readable qualification facade. Detailed machine rules remain in the taxonomy, seniority, location and qualification modules.

## Core profile

A strong opportunity normally has most of the following characteristics:

- South African hiring requirement;
- role maps to the authoritative Talent Tree role taxonomy;
- remuneration is above the normal R420,000 p.a. economic floor or is reasonably expected to be above it;
- role is senior, scarce, technically deep, strategically important or commercially valuable;
- employer is a current agreed client, agreed group entity, or an attractive target/prospect;
- vacancy is current or credibly active;
- evidence is strong enough to support action;
- relevant hiring ownership can reasonably be identified.

## Immediate-priority exception

A meaningful vacancy at a current agreed client or confirmed agreed group entity is automatically commercially important.

Agreed-client intelligence is retained even when a role falls outside the normal general-market taxonomy or economic filter, because the existing commercial relationship changes the opportunity value.

## Seniority interpretation

Do not equate seniority with people-management.

High-value opportunities include:

- board / Executive Director roles;
- C-suite and executive leadership;
- Director / Head / General Manager;
- Senior Manager;
- Principal / Lead / Architect;
- scarce Specialist;
- senior Consultant / Principal Consultant / Managing Consultant;
- fractional / interim / portfolio leadership;
- experienced professionals materially above the economic threshold.

Authority: `taxonomy/seniority_terms.yaml`.

## Relationship authority

Every opportunity must carry client status:

- `AGREED_CLIENT`
- `AGREED_GROUP_ENTITY`
- `PAST_CLIENT`
- `TARGET_PROSPECT`
- `UNKNOWN`

Authority: `sources/agreed-clients.yaml`.

## Important separation

Discovery may surface opportunities before all qualification facts are known.

Do not fabricate salary, employer identity, seniority, client status or vacancy freshness merely to force a record into the profile.
