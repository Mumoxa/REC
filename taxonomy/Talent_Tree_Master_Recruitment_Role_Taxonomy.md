# Classification rules

A few rules are critical because titles alone will create bad data.

**Functional IT vs general Business Analysis:** classify a Business Analyst under **Functional IT** when the work centres on applications, systems, technology implementation, requirements, integrations or digital platforms. Classify it as business/process work when it is fundamentally operating-model or process analysis.

**CRM Commercial vs CRM Technology:** a Lifecycle/Retention/CRM Strategy Manager belongs under Commercial CRM. A CRM Systems Manager, Salesforce Consultant or CRM Product Owner belongs under CRM Technology.

**Commercial vs Sales:** Commercial Finance, Pricing, Revenue Management, Category Management and Commercial Strategy are included. Direct revenue hunting, account acquisition and traditional sales/BD are excluded.

**Data vs BI:** data pipelines/platforms belong under Data Engineering. Analytical transformation belongs under Analytics Engineering. Dashboards/reporting belong under BI. Predictive/statistical modelling belongs under Data Science. Production models belong under ML/AI.

**Cloud vs Cybersecurity:** Cloud Engineers and Cloud Architects are included. Cloud Security is excluded.

**Risk vs Cyber Risk:** Enterprise, Operational, Financial, Credit, Compliance and Regulatory Risk remain included. Cyber Risk is excluded.

**Applications vs ERP:** generic business systems, CRM, applications analysis and implementation remain included. If the role exists primarily to implement/support SAP, Oracle ERP, Dynamics or NetSuite, it is excluded.

# Talent Tree search priority

The taxonomy should then be weighted approximately:

**Tier 1 — highest commercial priority**

C-suite, Managing Director, Director, Head, Executive, General Manager.

**Tier 2**

Senior Manager, Manager, Principal, Lead, Architect, highly scarce specialist.

**Tier 3**

Experienced professional/specialist roles comfortably above the R420k threshold.

The search engine should **not automatically downgrade scarce technology, data, actuarial, investment or functional-IT specialists simply because they do not manage people**. Scarcity, remuneration, technical depth and commercial value can place a Principal Data Engineer or Solution Architect above a conventional manager.

This gives us a **271-node detailed search taxonomy across 35 functional groupings**, with the unwanted ERP and Cybersecurity branches removed rather than merely marked low priority. This is the structure I would now use as Talent Tree's source-of-truth taxonomy for job discovery, market mapping, search strings, vacancy classification and candidate sourcing.
