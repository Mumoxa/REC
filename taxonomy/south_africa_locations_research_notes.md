# South Africa Location Research Notes

## Status

This file preserves the research rationale supplied with the authoritative location ontology.

Runtime geographic names and ordering live in:

`taxonomy/south_africa_locations.yaml`

The YAML file controls execution order. These notes explain why certain clusters receive disproportionate attention.

## P1 — Western Cape

### Northern Suburbs / N1 corridor

Particularly important for:

- finance;
- head-office;
- retail;
- FMCG;
- manufacturing;
- professional;
- shared-services roles.

Search the individual nodes rather than relying on "Cape Town" alone.

### Industrial Cape Town

Epping, Montague Gardens, Paarden Eiland, Airport Industria, Blackheath, Kuils River, Brackenfell Industrial, Stikland Industrial, Triangle Farm, Killarney Gardens, Maitland Industrial, Elsies River, Parow Industria and Atlantis must remain individually searchable.

User-supplied research source:
https://www.capetown.gov.za/work-and-business/planning-portal/spatial-analysis-and-research/economic-area-profiles/

### Stellenbosch / Winelands

Stellenbosch receives disproportionately high search attention because of its concentration of business-services, finance, agriculture, food, wine and tourism activity.

User-supplied research source:
https://www.westerncape.gov.za/treasury/files/wcg-blob-files?file=2026-03%2FMERO+Winelands+25-2026.pdf&type=file

## P2 — Gauteng

### Sandton / Johannesburg North

Treat as the first major professional-job cluster in Gauteng.

### Midrand / Waterfall

Search separately from Johannesburg rather than relying on metro-level coverage.

User-supplied research source:
https://joburg.org.za/about_/regions/Pages/Region%20A%20-%20Diepsloot%2C%20Midrand/business.aspx

### Ekurhuleni / East Rand

High importance for industrial, manufacturing, FMCG, logistics, engineering and supply-chain finance roles.

User-supplied research source:
https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2026/2026033101%20Cities%20Economic%20Outlook%20Report%202026.pdf

## P3 — KwaZulu-Natal

### Umhlanga / Durban North

First KZN cluster for head-office, finance, professional and corporate hiring.

### Pinetown / Westmead / New Germany

Major industrial/manufacturing search cluster.

User-supplied research source:
https://dag.durban.gov.za/uploads/0000/6/2025/09/19/ethekwini-industrial-spatial-strategy-report.pdf

### South Durban industrial belt

Mobeni, Jacobs, Clairwood, Prospecton, Isipingo, Amanzimtoti and Umbogintwini receive dedicated attention for manufacturing, chemicals, automotive, FMCG, logistics and industrial finance.

User-supplied research source:
https://economic.durban.gov.za/press-statement/EThekwini%2BReleases%2B17%2BPrime%2BSites%2BTo%2BBoost%2BInvestment%2BAnd%2BJob%2BCreation

### Hammarsdale / Cato Ridge

Give more weight than older broad KZN strategies normally do because the supplied research identifies this as an important industrial/logistics expansion corridor.

User-supplied research source:
https://sizakala.durban.gov.za/press-statement/new-hammarsdale-taxi-rank-set-to-drive-local-economic-growth-and-job-creation

## P4 — Rest of South Africa / national

After WC, Gauteng and KZN, rotate through the remaining provincial clusters and then national/remote/hybrid wording.

National terms are catch-all discovery terms. They do **not** prove granular geographic coverage.
