# Channel 2 — Agency Sites

## Architectural role

This is one of exactly four sourcing channels.

It is **Stage 2** and runs only after the agreed-client sweep.

## Mandatory agencies

- PRN Recruitment
- Communicate Recruitment
- Network Recruitment

Review the complete relevant current vacancy inventory of all three.

## Mission

Do not merely collect agency adverts.

For each relevant vacancy:

`Agency Advert → Extract Evidence → Identify Probable End Client → Verify Employer → Assess Commercial Value → Identify Hiring Stakeholders`

## Attribution effort

- executive / board / CFO / Director / Head / senior leadership: aggressive multi-angle research;
- senior / Lead / specialist / scarce skill: meaningful attribution effort;
- lower-priority roles: stop when employer attribution becomes disproportionate.

If the employer resolves to an agreed client or agreed group entity, promote immediately to the Channel 1 / Stage 1 commercial workflow.
