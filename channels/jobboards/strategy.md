# Job Board / ATS / Broader Secondary Discovery Strategy

## Architecture

Canonical channel facade:

`channels/job-boards.md`

This is **Channel 4 / Stage 4**.

Do not use this worker for the dedicated current agreed-client sweep. Agreed-client direct/indirect vacancy discovery belongs to Channel 1.

## Purpose

Search structured and governed broader-market vacancy sources after Channels 1–3 complete.

This channel covers:

- major South African job boards;
- non-client employer careers pages and ATS platforms;
- specialist/niche boards;
- public-sector / university sources where commercially relevant;
- the broader source-universe rotation;
- validated long-tail sources.

## Internal source order

Process:

1. Critical;
2. High;
3. Medium;
4. Low;
5. long-tail.

The detailed 350-source registry remains in the archived source map until compiled into a separate machine-readable source registry.

## Search completeness

A source is not "searched" merely because its homepage was opened.

For each assigned source perform one defensible action:

- review live inventory;
- use relevant filters;
- execute a source-specific search;
- or mark the source inactive/unavailable with evidence.

## Rotation

Do not attempt all 350 sources in every run.

Maintain governed state/cursor so the broader universe progresses rather than restarting from source 1 every day.

## Source preference

Where duplicates exist, prefer the earliest/original employer or recruiter source as canonical while retaining all other sources as evidence.

## Feedback loop

If a Stage 4 vacancy resolves to `AGREED_CLIENT` or `AGREED_GROUP_ENTITY`, promote it immediately into the Channel 1 / Stage 1 commercial workflow while preserving the original Stage 4 discovery provenance.

## Guardrail

High-volume public job boards must not crowd out or execute ahead of Channels 1–3.
