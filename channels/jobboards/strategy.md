# Job Board / ATS / Direct Careers Discovery Strategy

## Purpose

Search structured vacancy sources after applying the overarching agreed-client priority and source governance.

This worker is responsible for:

- official employer careers pages and ATS platforms;
- major South African job boards;
- direct employer portals;
- specialist/niche boards;
- public-sector / university sources where commercially relevant;
- broader source-universe rotation.

## Agreed clients first

Before broad-market scanning, inspect official career/ATS sources for agreed clients that expose direct vacancy pages.

Capture exact employing entity/business unit where relevant.

## Critical broad sources

The current source specification marks major sources including:

- PNet;
- Careers24;
- CareerJunction;
- Executive Placements;
- LinkedIn Jobs;
- Indeed South Africa;
- Google Jobs/search results;
- selected niche professional boards;
- priority recruitment agencies;
- direct employer portals.

The detailed 350-source registry remains in the archived source map until compiled into a separate machine-readable source registry.

## Search completeness

A source is not "searched" merely because its home page was opened.

For each assigned source perform one defensible action:

- review live inventory;
- use relevant filters;
- execute a source-specific search;
- or mark the source inactive/unavailable with evidence.

## Rotation

Do not attempt all 350 sources in every run.

Process in governed batches while preserving priority:

1. agreed-client direct sources;
2. mandatory priority agencies handled by agency worker;
3. Critical sources;
4. High;
5. Medium;
6. Low;
7. long-tail discovery.

Maintain a future persistent cursor/state so the broader universe progresses rather than restarting from source 1 each day.

## Source preference

Where duplicates exist, prefer the earliest/original employer or recruiter source as canonical while retaining the other sources as evidence.
