# Recruitment Agency Discovery Strategy

## Overarching rule

Agency adverts are commercially useful intelligence. Do not treat the recruitment agency as the hiring employer unless evidence says it is hiring for itself.

## Mandatory exhaustive sources

### PRN Staffing
Career opportunities: https://prnstaffing.co.za/career-opportunities/

Capture every potentially relevant live role, including reference, location, salary, function, requirements, systems, industry/company clues and reporting-line clues.

### Communicate Recruitment
Jobs: https://www.communicate.co.za/find-jobs  
Regions: https://www.communicate.co.za/job-regions

Search all current vacancies, not only adverts naming agreed clients.

### Network Recruitment
South Africa jobs: https://www.networkrecruitmentinternational.com/jobs-in-south-africa  
Regions: https://www.networkrecruitmentinternational.com/job-regions  
General: https://www.networkrecruitmentinternational.com/find-jobs  
Finance: https://www.networkrecruitmentinternational.com/finance-jobs

Search all current relevant South African vacancies.

## Client priority

Within agency discovery, first test whether a vacancy could plausibly belong to an agreed Talent Tree client, but do not bias attribution toward a client merely because a commercial agreement exists.

## Anonymous-client protocol

For hidden employers:

1. extract every identifying clue;
2. search exact title + location + distinctive phrase;
3. check agreed-client careers pages;
4. check LinkedIn posts / LinkedIn Jobs;
5. check duplicated wording at other agencies;
6. compare industry, location, company scale, ownership, ERP/systems, qualifications, reporting line, team size, operational scale, products/services;
7. classify employer as CONFIRMED, PROBABLE, HYPOTHESIS or UNKNOWN.

Research effort should scale materially with role seniority/value.

A Director/Head/Executive anonymous vacancy deserves deeper attribution than a low-value role.
