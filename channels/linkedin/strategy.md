# LinkedIn Discovery Strategy

## Core principle

Do not begin broad-market vacancy discovery with conventional job boards. Begin with hiring signals.

Treat LinkedIn posts, employee/company/executive activity, recruiter posts, team-growth signals, role announcements, leadership departures and business-expansion signals as a primary discovery layer.

For **agreed clients**, preserve the overarching client-first commercial priority and inspect the relevant agreed-client LinkedIn universe before the general market.

## Target classifications

- CONFIRMED_VACANCY
- PROBABLE_VACANCY
- MULTI_ROLE_HIRING
- EARLY_HIRING_SIGNAL
- EXECUTIVE_DEPARTURE
- BUSINESS_EXPANSION
- HIRING_AUTHOR
- REPOST
- AGENCY_ADVERT
- IRRELEVANT

## Signal families

Run signal families separately rather than relying on one phrase.

### Explicit hiring
`we're hiring`, `we are hiring`, `now hiring`, `currently hiring`, `actively hiring`, `we are recruiting`.

### Vacancy language
`vacancy`, `vacancies`, `open role`, `open position`, `job opening`, `role available`.

### Looking/searching
`looking for`, `we're looking for`, `searching for`, `seeking a`, `we need someone`.

### Join-the-team
`join our team`, `join my team`, `join us`, `come work with us`.

### Application CTA
`send your CV`, `CVs to`, `email your CV`, `apply now`, `applications open`.

### Informal contact CTA
`DM me`, `message me`, `reach out`, `get in touch`, `inbox me`.

### Team growth / function build
`our team is growing`, `expanding our team`, `building our team`, `build the function`, `establish the function`.

## Early signals

Do not classify as a confirmed vacancy without role evidence:

- executive departure / resignation;
- new office, plant, warehouse, branch or business unit;
- expansion into South Africa/Africa;
- acquisition, funding or major contract;
- team/function build.

## Search matrix

Use overlapping micro-searches:

**HIRING SIGNAL × ROLE/FUNCTION × GEOGRAPHY × RECENCY**

Search lanes:

1. signal + geography;
2. role first;
3. seniority first;
4. functional noun;
5. company watch;
6. hiring-signal author watch;
7. external LinkedIn X-ray.

Avoid a few enormous Boolean searches.

## Geography

Search national terms, province/metro names and commercial/industrial nodes. Include old/new municipal names, abbreviations and colloquial forms.

Priority metros include Cape Town/Western Cape, Johannesburg/Gauteng, Pretoria/Tshwane, Durban/KZN, Gqeberha/Eastern Cape, Bloemfontein, Mbombela, Polokwane, Rustenburg and Kimberley.

## Visual content

When commercially relevant LinkedIn content contains an image, PDF, carousel, graphic or screenshot, inspect the visual content where the runtime supports it.

Extract where possible:

- role;
- employer;
- location;
- salary;
- requirements;
- closing date;
- contact person;
- email;
- application URL;
- employment/work model.

## Reposts / social trail

Trace reposts to the original post where possible. Preserve both original author and useful amplifiers because either may be commercially relevant.

## Multi-role posts

One source post can create multiple vacancy records. Each vacancy must retain a common parent signal/source identifier.
